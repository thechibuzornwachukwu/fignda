Logo

fignda

The mark merges f and g into one stroke. The bowl of the g becomes a monocle, its lens lit in highlight. It is the moment you look closer. Drawn at Manrope ExtraBold weight. The wordmark is Manrope ExtraBold, lowercase, tracked tight.

Usage

Do
  • Let the puzzle be the loudest thing on screen.
  • Write short copy. One idea per line.
  • Separate with space and 1px lines.
  • Save the highlight for success.
Avoid
  • Gradients, glows and heavy shadows.
  • A second typeface or accent colour.
  • Pills and oversized radii.
  • Em dashes and exclamation marks.
