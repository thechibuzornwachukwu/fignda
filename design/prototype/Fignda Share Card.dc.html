Two shareables, one sheet

Tapping Share after a game opens one sheet. Pick the card, pick the frame, send. For Puzzle, choose Excerpt or Full puzzle. Result is the default because it is always safe. Puzzle shares the paragraph itself as a challenge. Show my finds is off by default. Turning it on highlights your words and stamps the card Contains answers, so nobody is spoiled by accident.

Excerpt picks whole sentences with the most hidden words and says how many more are inside. Full puzzle keeps the text readable and splits it into swipeable pages when it will not fit. Both link to the same puzzle. Daily puzzles lock Show my finds until the day ends.
