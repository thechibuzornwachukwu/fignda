01
Bury itFlagship idea

You make the puzzle. We give you three words. Write one normal sentence that hides all of them.

  1. 1Get three words. Everyone gets the same three today.
  2. 2Write a sentence that hides them. Writing a word plainly does not count.
  3. 3Other players try to find them. The longer they take, the more you score.

Word games have always had a setter and a solver. Here every player is both, and a good hider beats a fast finder.
