Gazecraft, new plan, 9 Oct 2026

Every sitting is a clue. Every chapter is a case.

One page for what we build, what we sell, what we refuse, and what nobody can copy. Detail and order of work stay in BUILD_PLAN.md. Behaviour stays in SPEC.md.

A few minutes of real detective work, in place of scrolling. Something is hidden in plain sight. Look closer and uncover it.

One skill across every game: looking closely. Words today, numbers and shapes next. It ends, it is worth doing, it respects you.

1. Player flow

From first visit to a daily habit

Play first, account later. Nobody is asked for anything before they have found something.

  1. ArriveA playable line, one guided find Live
  2. First clue3 to 5 minutes as a guest
  3. Keep this scoreFirst time sign in is offered Live
  4. This is youCharacter, outfit question, name Live
  5. Today's dailyOne paragraph, count hidden
  6. Done for todayMore is one tap away, never pushed

Each day after: the daily (5 minutes), then one stop on the case if the player wants it. Each week: circle board, league table, rest day if earned.

2. Game flow

One case, start to finish

A chapter is a case file. Each stop is one short sitting and gives one piece of the secret. The last stop puts the pieces together.

  • Word clue
  • Word clue
  • Number clue
  • Word clue
  • Shape clue
  • Unmask. Case closed

Rhythm is fixed, not random: word, word, number, word, shape. The whole puzzle stays as the hard one for players who want it.

  1. Open the caseOne line: what was hidden, and a masked culprit
  2. Sitting5 to 9 finds, saved if you leave, breaks cost nothing
  3. Piece earnedStars, a record, the case ring moves
  4. The secretBuilt by the engine from what you found
  5. UnmaskedThe culprit explains. Rank and outfit earned

3. The cast

Who is on the case, and who hides things

4 partners, and you

  • Detective X the cat (the logo), Detective Tobs the dino, Detective Puff the dog, Agent 404 the robot.
  • A player picks 1, free. The others are unlocked with points.
  • The partner reacts. The player's own avatar is the detective.

A new culprit each case

  • An ordinary character under a disguise, drawn with the avatar parts we have.
  • Tied to the chapter: a mascot head for football, a director for Nollywood.
  • Unmasking is the reward at the end.

One thread through all of them

  • Every culprit leaves the same calling card.
  • Someone taught them all to hide things. Never caught in a single case.

The tone rule

  • Motive is mischief, pride or a surprise. Never harm.
  • What is hidden is harmless: a trophy, a recipe, a hymn sheet.
  • For all ages. Accounts from 13. Younger players join on a parent's device or room.

Points: one currency, learned from Duolingo's XP

RuleWhat we doWhat it avoids
One currencyPoints only. They raise rank and fill the weekly table. No gems, no second coinAn economy nobody can explain
Skill pays moreBase points per find, plus fixed bonuses for a clean read, no hints and a rare findVolume beating skill
No farmingA first clear pays in full. A replay pays only the improvement. Unverified plays pay nothing LiveEasy puzzles repeated to win a table

No boostsNo double-points timers, no paid multipliers, no potionsGrinding against a clock, pay to win
A week has a ceilingWeekly points come only from dailies and first clears. Both are finiteSunday night sniping, and the cheating that Duolingo's open-ended tables drew
A skill number beside pointsSharp eye: the share of rare words you caught in your last 14 dailiesPoints that measure time spent. Duolingo is moving some courses from XP to a Score for this reason

Sponsored cases pay points at the same rate as any case. More to play is more to earn. A sponsor never buys a multiplier, so nobody ranks higher for playing a brand's case than for playing ours.

Together: rooms are a squad on one case Live, circles and friend streaks Live, player-made puzzles feed new cases once they pass the safety check, 20 players and 80% liked.

To decide

  • The plan lists a paid archive of past dailies. That clashes with "past cases stay free". Suggested: keep every archive free and drop it.
  • Prices for every stage, and what a sponsor may and may not write.
  • The numbers a sponsor will ask for: weekly players, plays per case, finish rate.

Data rules (GDPR, NDPA)

  • Sponsors get numbers, never people.
  • Play with no account. Accounts from 13.
  • Find data is kept as counts, not as people.
  • Private cases need a data agreement with the school or company.
  • Download my data, and delete, in Settings.
  • No ad cookies without consent. Ads come last.

Never for sale

  • Hints, extra tries, time, or anything that touches a score.
  • Streak freezes. Rest days are free.
  • Spins, chests, paid chances.
  • Anything that was free on the day a player joined.

8. Refusals

What we will not do

Claims

  • No word about memory, attention span, focus, IQ, brain training or "proven". Lumosity paid $2 million for that.
  • No "made by AI" and no "written by a person".

Pressure

  • No guilt or loss in reminders.
  • No content that expires. Seasonal cases stay.
  • No hearts or energy that lock a player out.

Content

  • No violence, gangs, weapons, romance or fear.
  • No puzzle shown before the safety check and the report rule.
  • No answer count typed by hand or taken from AI. The engine counts.

Scope

  • No dozen thin games. 2 new ones, done properly, then more.
  • No card-flip or reaction-speed games. Different skill.
  • No feature that does not replace or fold into an old one.
  • No under-13 accounts without legal advice.

10. Order of work

What to build, in this order

  1. Sell now. The "With NAME" mark, the pitch and price list, the sponsor report. Then ask 20 people.
  2. Sittings. Cut puzzles into passages, save and resume, clock counts time on the puzzle. 25 stops become about 55.
  3. Cases. Stops become clues, chapters become cases, the secret from the engine, culprits and unmasking, ranks.
  4. Buried sums, then Mirror. The first number game and the cheapest second word game, on the fixed rhythm.
  5. Holiday cases. Each with its own theme. Outfits and the archive ship with the first.
  6. Make takes over any-topic. One box, a preview, player-owned puzzles feeding the library.
  7. Server rewards. Rest days, crowns, skill badges, then leagues.
  8. One shape game. Odd one out or Trace.
  9. Private cases for schools, teams and churches, then supporter payment.
