# The long spoon

Case 1 of Lantern Row. A "who did it", 4 suspects. The story the chapter file is made from (`design/CASE_WRITING.md`).

## The truth

In 2011 the cook-off committee planed a name off the Long Spoon: R. MARLOW, said to have bought her stew base. Nobody proved it. Her son, Theo, was 14. He has photographed the last 9 finals with his back to the crowd.

The night before the 20th final:
- 6.40 pm. Theo takes key 7 from the store room hook "for the lights", and signs the key book in pencil, so the blotter will not keep it. The cabinet key hangs on the same ring, for the extension reels on the bottom shelf.
- He takes the spoon from the cabinet, locks it again, and burns R. MARLOW 2011 back into the planed patch with a soldering iron run off a reel. The room smells of burnt wood.
- He means to oil it and hang it back by 7 the next morning, before anybody looks. The burn takes longer than he thought.
- 9.15 pm. Key 7 goes back. The spoon is in his lamp case, wrapped in a towel. He tears the night's page from the key book, and misses that the blotter under it kept the ink of every other line.
- He pins a notice on the hall board, worded as a room to let so that only somebody reading closely sees it: her name goes back before the 20th is burned. Signed "her son". He wants it known, not yet by whom.
- He leaves on the empty hooks the card he was handed a week ago: a lantern drawn on it, and nothing else. He does not know who left it in his lamp case. Case 8 does.

## The ending

The spoon is in Theo's lamp case, with the soldering iron. The patch reads R. MARLOW 2011, in letters less even than the rest. He says she never bought that stew. The committee has until 6 to decide whether the 19th name stays. Mr Cole has not offered to fetch his plane.
